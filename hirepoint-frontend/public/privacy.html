HirePoint

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

HirePoint is a personal job application tracker built and run by Begli Rejepov. This page explains what information HirePoint collects, how it is used, and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Account details: the username you choose and your password.
  • Your applications: everything you enter for a job application, such as the company, job title, status, date applied, posting link, and notes.

HirePoint does not ask for your email address or real name, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are never stored as you typed them. They are stored as a one-way BCrypt hash, so not even the site owner can see your password.
  • All traffic to HirePoint is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Your applications can only be viewed or changed by your own account. The database is not reachable from the public internet.

How it is used

Your information is used only to run HirePoint: to log you in and to show you your own applications. It is never sold, rented, or shared with anyone for marketing.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on a server hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS Lightsail) in the United States, with automatic daily backups that are kept for up to 7 days.

Services that help run HirePoint

The website is delivered through Cloudflare and Amazon Web Services (S3 and CloudFront). Like any website, these services see technical information needed to deliver pages, such as your IP address and browser type. They process this under their own privacy policies.

Logs

The server keeps basic logs of account and application activity (for example, that a username created or deleted an application) to help find and fix problems.

Your login session

When you log in, HirePoint stores a login token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It expires after one hour, and logging out removes it.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and all of your applications, email beglir100207@gmail.com with your HirePoint username. You may be asked to confirm that the account is yours. Your data will be removed from the live database, and from backups as they expire within 7 days.

Children

HirePoint is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to beglir100207@gmail.com.
